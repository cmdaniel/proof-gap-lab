### Does speed close the gap?

A uniformly random input hits the high-value drift bug with probability **4.97e-13** (994,912,718,828 trigger (amount, balance) pairs out of about 2×10²⁴; the payee balance is irrelevant), so random sampling needs about **2.0e+12** samples on average.

| | samples tried | found? | rate | expected time to find |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Python | 2,000,000 | no | 0.7 M/s | 33.9 days |
| Rust | 1,000,000,000 | no | 161.8 M/s | 3.5 hours |

Z3 refutes it in 3.9 ms (Python) / 4.3 ms (Rust).
